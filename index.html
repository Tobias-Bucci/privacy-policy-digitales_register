  • examinations and assignments;

Depending on the institutional system, the App may process profile information such as:

To provide its functionality, the App may store information locally on the user's device, including:

5. Google Analytics for Firebase

The App may use Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the App is used and to improve its functionality and usability.

Depending on the configuration and platform, analytics data may include:

  • App interactions and events;
  • screens or features used;
  • session information;
  • App version;
  • operating system information;
  • device characteristics;
  • technical identifiers generated or processed by the analytics service where applicable.

The App operator does not intentionally send educational content such as grades, messages, passwords, or downloaded school documents to Firebase Analytics.

Analytics data may be pseudonymized or aggregated. Pseudonymized information may still constitute personal data under the GDPR.

Consent: Where required by applicable law, Google Analytics for Firebase is activated only after the user has provided the necessary consent. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR. Consent is voluntary and may be withdrawn with effect for the future through the privacy or consent settings available within the App.

6. Firebase Crashlytics

The App may use Firebase Crashlytics to identify crashes, technical errors, and stability problems.

Crashlytics may process technical diagnostic information such as:

  • crash reports and stack traces;
  • App version;
  • operating system version;
  • device type and architecture;
  • technical state of the App at the time of an error;
  • diagnostic identifiers;
  • other technical information necessary to analyze a crash.

The App operator does not intentionally include passwords, grades, messages, or other educational content in crash reports. Diagnostic information may be pseudonymized but should not necessarily be considered anonymous under data protection law.

Where required by applicable law, Crashlytics processing is performed only after the user has provided the necessary consent. The legal basis for consent-based processing is Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR.

7. Consent Management

The App may use Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) or other consent-management mechanisms to obtain and manage privacy choices where applicable.

Consent information may include technical information necessary to record and respect the user's privacy choices. Users may modify or withdraw consent through the privacy settings provided within the App where this functionality is available.

Consent to optional analytics or diagnostic processing is not required to use the App's essential educational functionality.
